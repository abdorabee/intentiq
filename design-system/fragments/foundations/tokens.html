Intent & status
The two families overlap on purpose: --card and --bg-elevated resolve to the same value, as do --background/--bg and --foreground/--text-primary. New React work should prefer the shadcn names (they are what @theme inline maps to Tailwind utilities like bg-card); the design-system names exist for the ~8,000 lines of ported semantic CSS in globals.css.
Do not edit the :root / .dark blocks near the top of globals.css. They still carry a stale violet oklch palette. theme-overrides.css is imported after globals.css in app/layout.tsx and wins by source order — edits upstream of it are silently discarded.
