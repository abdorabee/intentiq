Brand ramp
The one pairing that matters
--primary--primaryfilled action background
--primary-foreground--primary-foregroundtext on that background
--primary-foreground is black, not white. Yellow is a high-luminance hue: white text on #dfff00 lands near 1.1:1 and is unreadable. Never override this pairing.
Where the accent is allowed
Primary buttons, focus rings (--ring uses --brand-active), the active nav indicator, --chart-1, and credit meters. It is not a status colour — intent bands own HOT/WARM/COLD, and success/warning/danger are separate tokens.
