The one pairing that matters

--primary-foreground--primary-foregroundtext on that background
--primary-foreground is black, not white. Yellow is a high-luminance hue: white text on #dfff00 lands near 1.1:1 and is unreadable. Never override this pairing.
Fill vs. text — the rule
UseTokenWhere
Fills, borders, glows--brandbuttons, focus rings, meters, --chart-1
Text & icons--brand-inklinks, tinted icon glyphs, inline labels
--brand is #dfff00 in both themes. --brand-ink tracks it in dark mode, where yellow on near-black measures 18.4:1, and drops to a dark olive in light, where the raw brand would be 1.14:1 on white. Its worst case across #ffffff, #f7f8fa, #eef1f6 and the --brand-soft wash is 5.05:1.

Neither token is a status colour — intent bands own HOT/WARM/COLD, and success/warning/danger are separate tokens.
One deliberate exception: surfaces that are hardcoded dark in both themes — the .code-surface syntax highlighting and the onboarding wizard — keep raw #dfff00 as text. They never turn light, so the olive would be the wrong colour there.
