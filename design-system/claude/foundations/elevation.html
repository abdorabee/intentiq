Elevation & glass

Depth comes from a hairline ring plus a soft drop — not from a heavy shadow. In dark mode the ring is a white alpha; in light mode it is an ink alpha. The glow shadow is the only coloured one.

app/theme-overrides.css + app/globals.css @layer utilities
Shadow scale
--shadow-smhover lift, chips
--shadow-mddropdowns, popovers
--shadow-lgdialogs, command palette
Composite — ring + drop
--shadow-cardresting card
--shadow-elevatedraised panel
--shadow-screenlanding hero mock
Brand glow — sparingly
--shadow-glowfocus / active brand surface
The only coloured shadow in the system. Reserve it for a brand-filled element that needs to read as active — never as ambient decoration on neutral surfaces.
Glass
.glassblur(16px)
.glass-strongblur(24px)
Utilities from globals.css, with light-mode values restored in theme-overrides.css. .glass-nav is the sidebar variant. Glass needs something behind it — on a flat page background it just reads as a slightly lighter box.
