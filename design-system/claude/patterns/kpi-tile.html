The four-across strip at the top of the dashboard. A tinted icon, a 30px sans number, a mono delta and an optional sparkline. Collapses to two columns under 1100px.

.kpi-meta11px --text-quaternary
.kpi-spark22px tall, 2px gaps, bars max 6px wide
The number is the exception to "numbers are mono" — .kpi-num is deliberately sans at 30px, where Inter's tighter tracking reads better at a glance; the delta beside it stays mono so the two are distinguishable. Icon tints are semantic: --hot-bg for hot, brand for credits, --warm-bg for signals.
The credits and velocity icon tints uses --brand-ink, not --brand. #dfff00 as a foreground colour measures 1.14:1 on white. --brand-ink stays #dfff00 in dark mode and drops to a dark olive (--brand-ink) in light, where its worst case across every light surface is 5.05:1. Rule of thumb: --brand fills, borders and glows; --brand-ink text and icons.
