The active item is a wash, not a brand fill — with a dozen rows on screen a yellow block would dominate the page. Attention is carried by the counts and the HOT dot instead. In light mode theme-overrides.css replaces each white-alpha wash with an ink equivalent; a hardcoded rgba(255,255,255,…) in new sidebar CSS will be invisible on white.
The credits Top up link uses --brand-ink, not --brand. #dfff00 as a foreground colour measures 1.14:1 on white. --brand-ink stays #dfff00 in dark mode and drops to a dark olive (--brand-ink) in light, where its worst case across every light surface is 5.05:1. Rule of thumb: --brand fills, borders and glows; --brand-ink text and icons.
